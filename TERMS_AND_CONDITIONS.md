Last updated: 23 September 2026

## About Crypto Radar

Crypto Radar is an experimental, privately operated cryptocurrency research tool.
It collects market observations and news, calculates experimental signals, and
records subsequent price performance to evaluate those signals.

## Research only

Scores, AI assessments and alerts are experimental information. They are not
financial advice, recommendations to buy or sell, or guarantees of future returns.
Crypto Radar does not automatically execute trades. You remain responsible for
any decisions you make using its output.

## Availability and accuracy

The software is provided as available. Data, calculations and AI-generated text
may be incomplete, delayed or incorrect. Operation depends on third-party services
and may be interrupted. Features may change as the research develops.

## Google Drive access

The optional weekly-report feature uses Google authorization to upload a report
ZIP to a configured folder in the authorized account. The ZIP contains a database
snapshot, application configuration and a scoring source file. The application
checks the authorized account and destination folder and verifies uploaded files.

The current integration requests the full Google Drive OAuth scope, which grants
broader access than this reporting feature uses. It does not intentionally make
reports public. Access to uploaded files depends on the destination folder's
existing sharing settings.
