## Feed semantics and acquisition gates

* Coinbase Exchange `match.side` is the **maker** side: maker sell implies taker buy and maker buy implies taker sell. Reject unknown side values rather than reverse them by default. [Exchange channels](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/exchange/websocket-feed/channels) describe matches, heartbeat and level2 updates. Track per-product sequence/trade IDs; if continuity is missing, mark the interval incomplete, reconnect and rebuild from a fresh snapshot rather than treating absent updates as zero flow. The [public trade endpoint](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-trades) is a possible backfill route, but historical retention and completeness for the anchors above have not been established.
* Kraken spot v2 [trade](https://docs.kraken.com/exchange/api-reference/spot-websocket-v2/trade) reports taker side directly; messages may batch distinct trades and the optional snapshot is only the most recent 50. The [book](https://docs.kraken.com/exchange/api-reference/spot-websocket-v2/book) has a snapshot and depth choices; validate top-10 checksums and invalidate/resnapshot on mismatch. The [post-trade endpoint](https://docs.kraken.com/api/docs/rest-api/get-post-trade) supports time filters and up to 1,000 results, but its complete historical retention and side classification for this use remain unverified. Kraken account, jurisdiction, rate and market-data licensing rules must be checked before any pilot.
* For Uniswap v3 the [swap interface](https://github.com/Uniswap/v3-core/blob/main/contracts/interfaces/pool/IUniswapV3PoolEvents.sol) describes signed pool token balance deltas, not a centralized maker-side field. Identify token0/token1 from the verified pool, classify the asset entering or leaving the pool, and retain the original signs. Multi-hop routing, MEV, chain reorganizations, block finality and uncertain receipt times make this a different measure from CEX taker flow. Track block hash and log index, roll back orphaned blocks, and backfill missing ranges; do not equate block time with observation time. RPC provider limits, archive availability and usage rights are unresolved.

Coinbase's [market-data terms](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data) grant a restricted, revocable licence with conditions; internal storage, derived features and redistribution require a specific review under the applicable agreement. Do not infer a blanket right to publish raw data. Current documentation states Coinbase public REST limits of 10 requests/s/IP (15 burst); WebSocket documentation lists connection, message and per-product/channel subscription limits. No provider latency or historical availability was measured. Public access does not imply unlimited use or no jurisdiction restrictions.

## Proposed bounded pilot (not authorized to deploy)

1. After explicit separate approval and rights review, validate three Coinbase product identities and one Base pool against authoritative venue/chain metadata, including historical listing or creation intervals. Freeze the mapping with evidence and mark unresolved assets unmapped.
2. First implement an offline parser over synthetic fixtures: maker-side inversion, Kraken taker-side passthrough, unknown side rejection, duplicate trade IDs, sequence gaps, reconnect snapshots, checksum mismatch, pool token-order inversion and reorg rollback. No live credentials or trading methods.
3. A separately authorized read-only observation would begin with one VVV-USD product and a short fixed window. Store source/event/receipt UTC, product, sequence/trade ID and validity flags, with retention bounded by the licence. Record disconnects and never fill gaps with zeros. Record latency percentiles only from actual observations; compare to a predeclared completeness threshold before expanding.
4. Evaluate by asset and episode, with a frozen mapping and strict pre-anchor receipt cutoffs. Report uncovered assets, unavailable history, missingness and matched chronological controls; do not tune on the same holdout. The current study makes no claim of predictive lift, independence, complete history or production readiness.

The ranking is conditional: Coinbase first because the documented product primitives and three name-backed leads support a small comparison; Kraken second as an independent venue with promising current symbol leads but unverified identity, rights and historical coverage; the Base pool third for complementary on-chain activity after chain verification. It is not a measured venue-coverage ranking, and the order must be revisited if access, cost, licence, mapping or observed completeness fails the gates.
